Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

This personal rclone application accesses Google Drive on behalf of the single authenticated user only.

  • Data collected: None by this site. Google Drive files are accessed locally via rclone and are not transmitted to any server operated by us.
  • Data usage: Files are listed and transferred directly between your machine and Google Drive API. No analytics, no tracking.
  • Data sharing: No data is shared with third parties.
  • Permissions: The app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope to list and manage Drive files. You can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
  • Contact: For questions, open an issue at github.com/axillv/axillv.github.io.
